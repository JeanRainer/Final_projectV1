Japan

Credit: @brewculture

Japan is world-renowned for its meticulous tea ceremonies and high-quality green teas like Matcha and Sencha. Unlike Chinese teas which are often pan-fired, Japanese green teas are typically steamed to preserve their vibrant green color, deep earthy flavor, and nutrient profile.
