History

Exploring tea as culture, ritual, and quiet philosophy.

Brew Culture is a personal project dedicated to understanding tea beyond taste — as a reflection of history, geography, and human intention. From Chinese gongfu traditions to modern interpretations, this site explores how tea connects people across time and place.

China

They have a variety of tea processing methods.

Brew Culture aims to expand into regional studies, detailed brewing techniques, and cross-cultural comparisons, building a quiet archive of tea knowledge over time.

India

Milk and spices flavor their drink to energy.

This site exists to document and present tea culture in a way that is accessible yet respectful to its origins. It focuses on clarity, simplicity, and cultural depth rather than commercial trends.

Japan

They value tradition in honor.

The visual design is inspired by traditional East Asian aesthetics— soft tones, paper-like textures, and minimal structure. The goal is to create a calm reading experience, similar to observing a painting or preparing tea.
